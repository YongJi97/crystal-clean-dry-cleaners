Privacy policy

How we use the details you send when you request a pickup on the website or in the Crystal Clean app.

What we collect

A pickup request can include your name, phone number, email address (optional), pickup address, apartment or suite, preferred date and time, and any notes you add. The iPhone app can also save a profile and a history of your requests on that phone until you tap Clear my data.

Why we collect it

We use this information to schedule and complete your pickup and delivery, to contact you about that order, and to print a shop ticket. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Who receives it

  • Formspree receives the form so the shop gets an email. Formspree’s Discord plugin can also post an alert to the shop’s Discord.
  • Square receives the same request so we can create a $0 pickup order and print a ticket at the store.

The shop owners can also see the request on a private list used only to run pickups.

How long we keep it

We keep pickup details for as long as we need them to finish your order and for our shop records. Information saved in the app stays on your phone until you clear it or delete the app.

Ask us to delete it

Email crystalcleandrycleaners87@gmail.com or call 646-338-6883 and ask us to delete your pickup details. Clearing data in the app removes the copy stored on your phone. It does not by itself delete a request the shop has already received — contact us for that.
